PROMINENT DISCLOSURE: LOCATION DATA USAGE (BACKGROUND & FOREGROUND)

Krish Partner collects and processes location data to enable order assignment, real-time live order tracking for customers and store merchants, calculating delivery travel distance and estimated arrival time (ETA), and computing delivery partner earnings, even when the app is closed or not in use (background location).

Location tracking operates only while you are logged in and marked as "Online" / "On Duty" or actively handling an assigned order. You can pause or stop location tracking at any time by toggling your status to "Offline" / "Off Duty" or logging out of the app. Your location data is strictly used for order fulfillment and is never sold to third parties or used for behavioral advertising.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Krish Partner ("we," "our," "us," or "the App"), the official delivery partner application operated by Krish Delivery (accessible at https://krishdelivery.com). This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of information obtained from independent delivery personnel, couriers, and drivers ("Delivery Partners" or "you") who install and operate our mobile application (Package Name: com.krishdelivery.krishdeliveryboy).

By downloading, installing, registering with, or using Krish Partner, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

To register you as a legitimate Delivery Partner and assign order deliveries, we collect the following types of information:

2.1 Personal & Profile Information

  • Identity Data: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, profile photograph, and account credentials (password).
  • Onboarding & KYC Documents: Driving license copy and license number, vehicle registration number, vehicle category (bike, scooter, etc.), and identity documents (such as Aadhaar, PAN card, or Government ID) required for background screening, driver verification, and regulatory compliance.

2.2 Location Data (Foreground & Background)

  • Precise GPS Location: Real-time latitude and longitude coordinates, speed, bearing, and device movement collected during active order fulfillment.
  • Background Location Tracking: Continuous location tracking when the app is minimized or running in the background while your duty status is "Online" to dispatch nearby delivery requests and calculate accurate travel distance for partner payout.

2.3 Financial & Payout Information

  • Payout Details: Bank account number, Bank Name, IFSC code, or UPI ID provided by you solely for the electronic transfer of your delivery earnings, bonuses, tips, and incentives. We do not store sensitive payment card credentials (such as debit card CVV or ATM PINs).

2.4 Device & Technical Information

  • Device brand, model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (Android ID, IMEI), IP address, network connection status, battery level, app crash reports, and system performance telemetry.

2.5 Delivery Execution Data

  • Order pickup and delivery completion timestamps, route details, customer delivery confirmation signatures, and digital proof-of-delivery photos taken via the camera.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

The Krish Partner app requests specific device permissions strictly to perform delivery logistics:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: To identify your current location, route navigation, and track live order transit.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION: To enable real-time order dispatch and allow customers to track their delivery while your phone screen is off or you are using another navigation app.
  • CAMERA: To take photo proof of delivery when handing over packages and to photograph KYC identity documents during onboarding.
  • CALL_PHONE: To initiate calls to the customer or store merchant directly from the app regarding delivery instructions, gate access, or address clarification.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To deliver instant push notifications regarding incoming order requests, order cancellations, and payout updates.
  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep critical delivery synchronization alive during active transit without dropping GPS connection.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following essential business purposes:

  • Account Onboarding & Identity Verification: Validating your eligibility, driver's license validity, and vehicle documentation.
  • Smart Order Dispatch: Matching delivery requests with the nearest available partner to reduce transit time and optimize fuel/travel efficiency.
  • Live Customer & Merchant Tracking: Displaying your first name, phone number, and live map location to the customer and store merchant while an order is in transit.
  • Payouts & Compensation: Calculating per-kilometer travel allowances, delivery fees, bonus incentives, and disbursing direct earnings to your bank account or UPI ID.
  • Safety & Dispute Resolution: Resolving missing item claims, customer delivery disputes, and preventing fraud or unauthorized account sharing.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Satisfying commercial transport, tax, and accounting reporting requirements.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We value your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is only shared in the following authorized contexts:

  • With Customers and Merchants: During an active delivery only, the customer and merchant are provided your first name, phone number, vehicle details, and live GPS map location. This sharing automatically ceases immediately upon order completion.
  • Google Maps & Location Services (Google LLC): To provide map rendering, route optimization, and address geocoding. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): To dispatch real-time order notifications and alert sounds.
  • Banking & Payment Partners: Authorized financial institutions and payment gateways to execute partner earnings transfers.
  • Law Enforcement & Regulatory Authorities: When required by statutory law, court orders, or official law enforcement investigations.

6. Data Security & Storage

We apply robust physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal data. All data transmissions between the Krish Partner app and our backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Partner KYC documents and financial details are kept in secured database environments with restricted administrative access.

7. Data Retention

We retain your profile, onboarding records, and payout history as long as your Delivery Partner account is active. Location trail data is periodically purged after order settlement and audit windows close. Financial transaction records are retained for the statutory period required under Indian financial and taxation laws.

8. Partner Rights & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policies, delivery partners retain complete control over their personal information and may request full account deactivation and data deletion at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

To permanently delete your Krish Partner account and all associated personal data, submit an email request from your registered email address to support@krishdelivery.com with the subject line "Delete Delivery Partner Account". Please provide your registered mobile number and full name.

Upon receiving and verifying your request:

  • Your partner account will be immediately deactivated and logged out across all devices.
  • Your personal profile details, contact information, uploaded KYC document images, and location history will be permanently deleted from our active databases within 30 days.
  • Any outstanding delivery payouts will be finalized and cleared to your registered bank account.
  • Historical tax invoice and payout ledgers required under Indian financial compliance laws will be retained in encrypted cold storage for the statutory retention period.

9. Children's Privacy

The Krish Partner application is strictly restricted to authorized delivery personnel who are at least 18 years of age and legally eligible to hold a commercial or motor vehicle driving license. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from minors.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our operational practices, new feature launches, or evolving legal frameworks. In the event of material modifications, we will notify delivery partners via an in-app notice, push notification, or an updated revision date at the top of this policy page.
